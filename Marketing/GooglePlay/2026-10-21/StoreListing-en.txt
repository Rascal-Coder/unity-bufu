App title
TycoonRush

Short description
Flip coins, evolve your fortune, and build a team of powerful helpers.

Full description
Every flip is another step toward a bigger fortune. Tap to toss your coins, watch the income burst, and grow your collection in TycoonRush.

Flip Your Fortune
Fill the field with coins and enjoy the rush of critical-hit rewards. Invest your earnings to keep your fortune growing.

Build Your Team
Recruit distinctive helpers with their own skills. Unleash star magic, heart projectiles, tentacles and rockets as your team tosses more coins. Upgrade and awaken helpers to develop their abilities.

Upgrade Your Coins
Grow beyond bronze. Collect silver, gold and purple coins, strengthen their value, and evolve them into increasingly ornate forms.

Explore Two Worlds
Build your wealth in the Coin World, then unlock the Dice World for a new way to grow. Develop each world's upgrades and talents as you progress.

Flip. Upgrade. Build your team. How far will your fortune go?
